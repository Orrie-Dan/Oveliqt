# Brand Voice

> These are starter defaults for a software services company. Edit them to sound like you.
> The best improvement you can make is pasting 3–5 real posts or messages you've written under "Samples".

## Personality
- Clear, practical, confident and never hype-y
- Talks like a senior engineer explaining things to a smart business owner
- Helpful first: teaches something useful in every post and only sells sometimes

## Oveliq's voice (taken from the website copy)
- **Problem first, technology second:** "Technology is the tool. The outcome is the point."
- **Honest, even when that means less work for Oveliq:** "We'll tell you when ordinary software is the better answer."
- **Short, punchy statements, often in threes:** "Design. Engineering. Intelligence." / "Clear scope. Clear cost. No surprises."
- **Grounded in everyday work:** "the small frustrations that become someone's whole day"
- **Precise about what was really done:** says "partner contribution" and "independent prototype" plainly and never overclaims. Posts must do the same.
- Phrases worth reusing: "Built around real problems", "Software that moves", "Let's build something useful."

## Do
- Use plain language and explain any jargon in one line
- Lead with the reader's problem, not with "we"
- Use specific examples over general claims
- Keep sentences short, with one idea per paragraph on social posts
- End with one clear next step

## Don't
- Buzzwords: "synergy", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "leverage", "game-changer", "unlock"
- Fake urgency or clickbait
- Invented stats or client stories
- More than 2 emojis per post, or more than 3 hashtags

## Content mix (rough weekly target)
- 40% educate (how-tos, mistakes to avoid, explainers)
- 25% proof (projects, behind the scenes, process)
- 20% opinion and trends (takes on industry news)
- 15% direct offer (services, booking a call)
